Bio

I'm Jivika, a Computer Engineering student at Amrutvahini College of Engineering, Sangamner (affiliated to SPPU), currently in my 3rd year. My coursework covers HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Java, Python and PHP, and I use personal projects like this portfolio to go deeper into the parts of the web platform that a classroom syllabus can't fully cover — semantic markup and accessibility chief among them.

I care about building pages that work for everyone: fast on a slow connection, legible at any zoom level, and fully usable with a keyboard or screen reader — not just a mouse and good eyesight.
